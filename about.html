About Bseb Prep

Bseb Prep is built for one reason — Bihar Board students deserve a preparation tool that matches how the exam is actually set, not a generic app repurposed for every board in the country.

Why We Built This

Built by Intangible Network

Bseb Prep is developed and maintained by Intangible Network, a team focused on regional, exam-specific educational products rather than one-size-fits-all learning apps.

Bihar Board First

Every feature — from the objective-heavy quiz format to the PYQ structure — is designed around how BSEB actually sets Class 10 and 12 papers, not adapted from a CBSE or generic template.

Built With Students

Content, quiz difficulty, and formula cards are refined based on real student feedback and exam trends, updated every session.

Our Mission

Accessible

Quality exam preparation shouldn't depend on expensive coaching — Bseb Prep keeps core PYQ and quiz content free.

Accurate

Every question and note is checked against the actual Bihar Board exam pattern and marking scheme.

Distraction-Free

No clutter, no unrelated content — just what a student needs to walk into the exam hall prepared.
