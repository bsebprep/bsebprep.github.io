Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2025

BSEB PREP (“we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, profile picture, and class / grade level.
  • Device Information: Device IDs (Advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI), IP address, and device model.
  • Location Information
  • App Usage and Crash Data: Through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Payment Information: Through Razorpay.
  • Media: Photos selected by users for cropping or editing through UCrop.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Account registration and login
  • Personalized educational content
  • Analytics, app performance monitoring, and bug fixing
  • Push notifications and important updates
  • Advertising and personalized ad targeting
  • Payment processing for premium features

3. Third-Party Services and SDKs

Our app integrates with third-party services which may collect, process, and share your data according to their own privacy policies.

  • Firebase (Google): Privacy Policy
  • Google Cloud: Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Privacy Policy
  • AppLovin SDK: Collects device identifiers for advertising and analytics.
  • Unity Ads: Collects device identifiers for ad personalization.
  • Facebook Audience Network: Collects device and app information for personalized advertising.
  • InMobi & Vungle: Collect device and usage data for analytics and advertising.
  • Razorpay: Collects payment information for processing transactions.
  • UCrop: Accesses selected images only for cropping and editing.

4. Children Under 18

Our app is designed for students under 18 (Grades 9–12). Users under 13 years of age are not permitted to use this app.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to safeguard your data through secure Google Cloud and Firebase infrastructure. All sensitive information is transmitted securely using encryption.

6. Your Rights

You may request to view, update, or delete your data by contacting us. You can also opt out of analytics or notifications through the available app settings.

7. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we may notify you through in-app alerts or email.

8. Contact

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at bsebprep@hotmail.com.
